# Earned Autonomy

Earned Autonomy treats time as an evidence structure rather than a promise. Space is divided by a visible structural grid; phases occupy measured spans, dependencies cross only where causality requires them, and every milestone is anchored by a proof gate. The composition is quiet, exact, and editorial. It must feel meticulously crafted by someone who understands both executive decisions and production engineering.

White paper and black ink carry nearly all meaning. One electric blue marks the critical path and moments where capability becomes usable; red appears only at a no-go boundary, and green only where evidence has been earned. Typography provides the hierarchy: disciplined display sans for outcomes, an italic serif for the governing thesis, and monospaced labels for dates, gates, owners, and measures. Every alignment should reveal painstaking attention and master-level execution.

Rhythm comes from repeated month columns, hairline rules, and precisely weighted phase bars. The timeline is not decorative: its geometry communicates overlap, sequencing, and constraint before a word is read. Large fields of negative space keep the roadmap authoritative rather than busy. Text remains sparse and operational - outcomes, deliverables, exit criteria, and decisions - never prose added to fill space.

Each phase page behaves like a forensic plate from one larger system. A dominant numeral establishes scale; a small timeline locates the reader; three aligned evidence fields show what is built, how it is falsified, and what unlocks next. The result should appear labored over through countless refinements, with no accidental spacing, arbitrary color, rounded softness, or visual noise.

The subtle reference is a release train whose cars cannot move because a calendar says so; each coupling closes only when evidence is present. The final artifact should make that control logic intuitively visible. It is an executive roadmap, but it carries the craftsmanship and precision of a production control plane.
