## Cost and decision

Requirement 5 remains at **45%**. The harness, pre-registration, model attribution, and refusal
paths are proven; the experiment is not. Lowering the margin, raising the allowed zero rate, or
counting skipped/rate-limited calls would turn service availability into a false prompter result.

No production code, gate, detector, threshold, or parity fixture was changed. This B6 item should
be marked `[!]` by the wrapper as blocked on a reliable lane, not marked complete from the short
probe.
