## Assessment

The remediation was substantive, not cosmetic. Every prior P0 was fixed at the root — the
blueprints now name types that exist, with the fields they actually have, in the crate that
actually owns them — and 10 of 12 P1s went with them. `EDGE-TYPES.md` and `FR-TRACE.md` are the
right two artifacts to have added, and `FR-TRACE.md`'s willingness to publish its own 7 unowned FRs
rather than claim 46/46 is the single best thing in this change.

The failure is a consistency one: `EDGE-TYPES.md` was written as the authority and then not
propagated, so the artifact introduced to end cross-node disagreement is now itself the largest
source of it. Three of the four probe files also missed a fix their sibling received. Both are the
signature of a fix applied per-file instead of per-invariant — which is the same shape as
`fleet/PRINCIPLES.md` E1: the lesson was written down and then not transferred. Re-verify by
invariant, not by file, and this passes.
