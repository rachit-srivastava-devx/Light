## B4 — `fleet plan` missed ordinary user phrasing

The front door had four deterministic intents, but its vocabulary was narrower than the users'
language. I collected **25 prompts** in `tests/fixtures/plan-prompts.txt`: 20 in-scope fleet tasks
covering change, ledger verification, quota, and diagnosis, plus five out-of-scope requests. The
denominator is the 25 non-empty fixture lines.

I measured the baseline with the real binary using:

    FLEET_STATE="$PWD/var/fleet" ./keel/target/debug/fleet plan "<fixture line>"

The baseline was **17/25 sensible routes**, **2/25 refusals with useful candidates**, and **6/25
useless refusals**. A useful refusal means an in-scope request refused by the table but whose three
near-match examples included the expected intent family. The useful refusals were `introduce a
dry-run mode` (an implementation candidate was offered) and `quota ... available` (a quota
candidate was offered). The other in-scope miss (`why did ... fail`), plus all five out-of-scope
requests, had no relevant candidate.

The table now adds only bounded phrases: `introduce a/an` for changes, `available` when paired with
quota/tokens, and a diagnosis condition requiring a question form plus a failure symptom. The
unqualified `make ` verb remains absent. After the change the same command produced:

    TOTAL routes=20 refusals=5 denominator=25

That is **20/25 sensible routes**, **0/25 useful refusals**, and **5/25 useless refusals**: three
avoidable refusals became routes, and the useless-refusal count fell from 6/25 to 5/25. All five
out-of-scope prompts still refused, including:

    fleet: no committed intent matches: "Make me a sandwich"
      closest intents (3 candidates):
        fix the parser bug
        diagnose fleet
        is the ledger ok

The focused intent tests pass, including the three new phrasings and the sandwich refusal. The
required mutation command was also run, but it did not reach mutation measurement: cargo-mutants
failed its unmutated baseline after a 343-second isolated build and returned exit 4, surfaced by
`bin/mutants-gate.sh` as exit 6. That is an environment/measurement limitation, not a mutation
score; the full verifier was the final gate.

The final required command, `FLEET_MUTANTS=0 bash verify.sh`, returned exit 6 with **14 passed,
1 failed, 1 skipped (denominator: 16 stages)**. `fmt` is green after formatting; the remaining red
stage is `corpus`, outside the B4 files and unchanged by this slice. B4 therefore does not claim a
green full-repository gate.
